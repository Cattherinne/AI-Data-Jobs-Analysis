# Key Findings

## 1. Most Common Job Titles

- Data Scientist is the most common job title, with 709 job postings, followed by Data Engineer with 682 and Data Analyst with 627.
- Engineering-related roles are highly represented among the top 10 job titles, including Machine Learning Engineer, AI Engineer, Analytics Engineer, MLOps Engineer, and LLM Engineer.

## 2. Jobs by Industry

- Technology has the highest number of job postings, with 1,528 jobs, accounting for approximately 30.6% of the dataset.
- Finance is the second-largest industry with 839 postings, followed by Healthcare with 543.
- Energy and Education have the fewest job postings among the listed industries, with 176 and 196 respectively.

## 3. Top Countries by Number of Jobs

- The United States has the highest number of job postings, with 1,786 jobs.
- India ranks second with 782 postings, followed by the United Kingdom with 488.
- The US and India together account for approximately 51.4% of all job postings in the dataset.

## 4. Average Experience by Industry

- Government has the highest average required experience, at 8.98 years.
- Consulting and Finance also show relatively high average experience requirements, at 8.57 and 8.53 years respectively.
- Education has the lowest average experience requirement at 7.88 years.

## 5. Fully Remote Jobs by Industry

- Technology has the highest number of fully remote job postings, with 462 positions.
- Finance ranks second with 228 fully remote jobs, followed by Healthcare with 153.
- Technology accounts for approximately 32.5% of all fully remote positions in the dataset.

## 6. Jobs by Experience Level

- Mid-level positions are the most common, with 1,613 job postings.
- Senior-level positions are the second most common, with 1,520 postings.
- Entry-level positions account for 985 jobs, while Lead and Executive positions are less common.

## 7. Jobs by Region

- North America has the highest number of job postings, with 2,136 jobs.
- Europe follows with 1,502 postings, while Asia accounts for 912.
- Oceania and South America have considerably fewer postings, with 240 and 210 respectively.

## 8. Most Common Job Title in Each Industry

- Data Scientist is the most common job title in Technology, Healthcare, Government, and Retail.
- Data Engineer is the leading job title in Consulting, Education, and Finance.
- Data Analyst is the most common title in Manufacturing and Media.
- Machine Learning Engineer is the leading title in Energy.
- Media has a tie between Data Analyst and Data Scientist, with 38 postings each.

## 9. Percentage of Fully Remote Jobs

- Fully remote positions account for 28.40% of all job postings in the dataset.
- This indicates that while remote work represents a significant share of the job market in the dataset, the majority of positions are not fully remote.

## 10. Experience Level Distribution

- Mid-level positions represent the largest share of the dataset at 32.26%, followed by Senior positions at 30.40%.
- Together, Mid and Senior positions account for 62.66% of all job postings.
- Entry-level positions represent 19.70%, while Lead positions account for 13.12%.
- Executive positions make up the smallest share at 4.52%.
